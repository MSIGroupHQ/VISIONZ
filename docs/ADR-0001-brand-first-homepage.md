# ADR-0001 — Brand-first homepage correction

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-16

## Context
The first live Cloudflare render was rejected by the operator as visually poor. The source over-weighted oversized generic editorial typography and under-weighted the actual VISIONZ orbital mark and supplied garment photography.

## Decision
The homepage is rebuilt around the approved VISIONZ mark and supplied product/lookbook assets:

- the orbital VISIONZ mark is the dominant hero object;
- `SEE BEYOND.` is supporting copy, not a substitute logo;
- the supplied dark lookbook image becomes the first campaign surface;
- the confirmed See Beyond Shorts become the primary commerce surface;
- the supplied hoodie image becomes the manifesto/supporting campaign surface;
- no stock imagery, gradient decoration, social-feed export, or generic card grid is introduced;
- navigation remains rail/text-first with one visible Shop action;
- footer remains restrained and separates shop, support, legal, and operator information.

## Consequences
This intentionally changes the visual baseline. Future major changes to the hero, navigation, product presentation, or footer should be compared against this baseline and recorded in a new ADR when they materially change the brand system.
